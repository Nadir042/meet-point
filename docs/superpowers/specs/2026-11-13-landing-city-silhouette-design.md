# Landing page city silhouette design

## Scope

Replace only the Paris and Sydney background assets on landing-page sections two and three. Keep the existing copy, illustrations, layout, spacing, scroll behavior, and warm cream-to-coral background unchanged.

## Visual direction

Match the first section's city language: broad, low-detail architectural silhouettes with a light gray halftone grain. The city layer must remain quiet enough to sit behind content while still being recognizable.

- Paris: Eiffel Tower, low Haussmann roofline, and a restrained Seine silhouette.
- Sydney: Sydney Opera House, Harbour Bridge, and a restrained waterfront skyline.
- Palette: warm neutral gray only; no black, blue, or baked-in orange/pink background.
- Surface: transparent background with subtle stippled grain.
- Detail: strong landmark contours, few interior lines, no photorealistic texture, no generic geometric building blocks.
- Content safety: no text, logos, flags, people, or watermarks.

## Integration

Generate two new transparent raster assets and replace the existing Paris and Sydney image references without changing their containers. Preserve the current responsive `object-fit`, opacity, and layering unless a small opacity adjustment is required to match the first section.

## Success criteria

1. Paris and Sydney are identifiable at a glance on desktop and mobile.
2. Both images read as silhouettes from the same visual family as the first section.
3. Body copy and foreground illustrations retain stronger contrast than the city layer.
4. Existing section composition and scrolling behavior do not move.
5. Images load without errors through the local server.
