# Landing logo glass design

## Scope

Apply a small glass surface only to the `Meet.Point` logo in the landing-page header. Do not change the workspace logo, footer logo, navigation layout, copy, or header dimensions.

## Behavior

- At the top of the page, the logo sits on a very transparent rounded glass capsule with a faint border.
- After scrolling, the existing condensed header state strengthens the logo capsule's blur, border, and soft shadow.
- The transition follows the header's current scroll timing and motion preferences.

## Visual constraints

- Keep the current warm cream/coral palette and original logo lettering.
- Use a compact capsule with restrained padding; it must not read as a button.
- Preserve legibility against all three landing-section backgrounds.
- Avoid opaque white fills, heavy outlines, or a second competing orange accent.

## Success criteria

1. The logo remains aligned with the existing navigation on desktop and mobile.
2. The glass effect is visible but quieter than the header container and primary action.
3. The top and scrolled states transition without layout shift.
4. Keyboard and pointer behavior of the home link remains unchanged.
